No buy ticket yet

German mission: you don't need a paid flight ticket to apply for a visa

The German Mission in Nigeria said on X on 18 November that applicants do not have to buy a flight ticket before applying for a German visa. Depending on the visa checklist, a flight reservation or a proposed travel itinerary is enough. The post was the last in its series busting claims made by visa agents. Read more

UKStudents

Free master'sCommonwealth Shared Scholarships for UK master's close 8 December

Applications are open for the 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarships, which pay for a one-year master's at a UK university starting in September or October 2027, and Nigerians are eligible. The award covers full tuition, a return flight and a monthly living allowance of £1,712, or £2,000 in the London area. The closing time is 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026.

USAStudents

Internship reprieveUS judge blocks new limits on student internships (CPT)

A federal judge in Boston has stopped the Department of Homeland Security from enforcing two August 2026 directives that narrowed curricular practical training (CPT), the work placements F-1 students do as part of their course. Under the directives, a placement only counted if every student in the programme had to do it, which shut out elective internships taken for credit. The order is a preliminary injunction, so the case continues and the government could still appeal.

AustraliaRule change

No switchingAustralia visitor visas lodged from 28 November will carry 'No Further Stay'

From 28 November 2026, condition 8503 ('No Further Stay') becomes compulsory on Australian Visitor (subclass 600) tourist and business visas, as well as ETA and eVisitor visas. Anyone holding such a visa will generally be unable to apply for another visa, such as a student or work visa, while inside Australia. It only covers applications lodged on or after 28 November, and a waiver is possible only after a major change in circumstances you could not have foreseen.

USAStudents

Fewer arrivalsNew international students at US universities down 15% this autumn

The Institute of International Education says US colleges hosted about 1.15 million international students in 2025/26, but its autumn 2026 snapshot of more than 800 institutions shows total international enrolment down 9% and new enrolment down 15%. The fall in graduate numbers came mainly from fewer master's students. Most Nigerians cannot get new US student visas while the January 2026 suspension remains in place.

USAStudents

Court don ruleUS court strikes down four-year cap on student visa stays

A federal judge in Massachusetts has thrown out the Homeland Security rule that would have given F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors and foreign journalists a fixed admission period instead of 'duration of status'. The court had already paused the rule in September, and on 16 November it cancelled it completely. For now, students can stay as long as they keep studying full time on a valid I-20, but the government can still appeal.

UKVisa warning

No guaranteeBritish High Commission warns Nigerians: nobody can guarantee a UK visa

The British High Commission in Nigeria has told applicants that no agent, person or company can promise a successful UK visa. It says any offer of guaranteed approval by email, phone call or social media is a scam. Applications should only be made through the official GOV.UK website.
