Study route

Affordable schools that take Nigerians

Studying is still the most common legal way to japa. These are lower-cost options with clear entry rules. No school can promise you a visa, so check the fee for your exact course on the school's own website.

Many UK universities accept a WAEC or NECO credit in English instead of IELTS. Ask the admissions office before you pay for a test. Some also offer January intakes and first-year fee discounts.

🇩🇪 Germany: almost free tuition

Public universities in 14 of 16 states charge non-EU students no tuition, only a semester fee of about €70–€430. Baden-Württemberg charges €1,500 a semester, and TUM in Bavaria charges more.

Many master's courses are taught in English. You will need a blocked account of about €934 a month for a student visa.

🇨🇦 Canada: degree or college

Bachelor's and master's graduates can get a post-graduation work permit in any field. College diploma and certificate graduates only qualify if their programme is on the official eligible list, mainly health, trades, STEM, education and transport.

Check the school is a Designated Learning Institution and the course is work-permit eligible before paying a deposit.

🇮🇪 Ireland: English-speaking EU

Show about €10,000 a year for living costs plus proof your tuition is paid. You can work 20 hours a week in term and 40 in holidays. Graduates of degree-level courses can stay up to 2 years to work full-time.

Visa fee €60, plus €300 to register once you arrive.

🇦🇺 Australia: apply from home

From 2 October 2026 most people can't switch to a student visa inside Australia, and families are harder to bring along. Apply from Nigeria with the full family plan decided early.

More countries to consider

🇫🇷 France

Public universities now charge non-EU students €2,895 a year for a bachelor's and €3,941 for a master's, from September 2026. That is still far cheaper than the UK. Apply through Campus France Nigeria.

Fee waivers are limited to 10% of students, mostly scholarship holders and priority fields like health and IT.

🇮🇹 Italy

Public university fees are low and often based on family income. Regional DSU scholarships can cover tuition, meals and housing for students from lower-income families.

DSU deadlines usually fall in summer, before the course starts. Check each region's portal.

🇳🇿 New Zealand

Show NZ$20,000 a year for living costs on top of tuition. Students can now work 25 hours a week in term and full-time in holidays. Graduates can get an open post-study work visa.

Main intakes are February and July.

🇰🇷 South Korea

Universities usually ask for about US$20,000 in the bank. The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) pays tuition, flights and a monthly allowance. After graduating, the D-10 job-seeker visa now allows up to 3 years to find work.

Korea banned 20 universities from sponsoring student visas from fall 2026. Check your school isn't one of them.

🇯🇵 Japan

Your school applies for a Certificate of Eligibility for you first, then you get the visa at the Japanese embassy in Abuja. The MEXT scholarship covers fees and living costs and is applied for through the embassy.

Most courses need Japanese. Intakes are April and October, so start about 6 months early.

Other legal routes

Skilled work

UK Skilled Worker, Canada Express Entry and Germany's Opportunity Card suit graduates with work experience. See the route cards above.

Health professionals

Registered nurses, doctors and pharmacists still have strong routes to the UK, Canada and Australia once they pass the local licensing exam.

Family

Spouses and children of people with settled status can apply to join them. Each country sets its own income and English rules.

Scholarships

Chevening (UK), DAAD (Germany) and Commonwealth scholarships cover fees and living costs. Applying is free, so never pay anyone to "secure" one.

Japa wise

Best time to apply

Apply too late and you miss your course or trip. Apply too early and the embassy won't accept it. These are the usual windows.

🇬🇧 UK Student

Apply up to 6 months before your course starts. Aim for at least 3 months before, because June to September is peak season and slows everything.

🇬🇧 UK Visitor

Apply up to 3 months before you travel. Avoid applying in the busy summer and December holiday rush.

🇪🇺 Schengen

Apply up to 6 months before travel and no later than 15 days before. Appointments in Lagos and Abuja fill up early for summer, so book in spring.

🇨🇦 Canada Study

Apply as soon as you have your acceptance letter. September intake applications pile up from May to August.

🇺🇸 USA

Routine visas for Nigerians are now processed only in Lagos, and many visitor and student visas are suspended. Check the latest news above before paying any fee.

🇯🇵 Japan & 🇰🇷 Korea

Japan starts in April and October; Korea in March and September. Schools need about 6 months to process papers, and GKS embassy applications usually open early in the year.

🇳🇿 New Zealand

Intakes are February and July. Apply at least 3 months ahead, as visa queues build before February.

🇫🇷 France & 🇮🇹 Italy

Start in the autumn or winter before you want to begin. Campus France and Italian pre-enrolment deadlines usually close by spring.

🇦🇺 Australia Student

Apply from outside Australia, early. New rules from 2 October 2026 stop most people switching to a student visa inside the country.

Overstay wahala

No overstay your visa

Overstaying follows you for years. It shows up in every future application, in the UK, the US, Canada and across Europe.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

1–10 year ban

Overstay more than 30 days and you face a re-entry ban. How long depends on how you leave. Since March 2026 Nigeria accepts UK travel letters, so removals happen faster.

🇺🇸 United States

3 or 10 year bar

More than 180 days of unlawful stay brings a 3-year bar. A year or more brings a 10-year bar.

🇨🇦 Canada

Return permission needed

After a removal order you may need special permission to come back. Overstaying also kills future visa and PR applications.

🇪🇺 Schengen

Entry ban

Overstays are recorded in a database shared by 29 countries. Fines and bans can follow, and refusals become far more likely.

If your visa is about to expire, speak to a regulated immigration adviser before it runs out, not after.

Scam alert

How to spot fake visa promises

Nigerians lose billions of naira every year to fake agents. If you see any of these, walk away.

  • ✕"Buy CoS" or "sponsorship for sale"Real UK sponsors don't sell certificates. Paying for one can get you banned.
  • ✕Care worker jobs from NigeriaThe overseas route is closed. Any agent charging for it is collecting free money.
  • ✕Borrowed proof of fundsCanada and the UK check where money came from. Lent money that disappears after the visa leads to refusal and a record.
  • ✕"Guaranteed visa" agentsNo agent can guarantee a visa. Only the embassy decides.
  • ✕Fake job offers asking for feesGenuine employers don't ask you to pay for interviews, medicals or "processing".
  • ✕Paying into a personal accountVisa fees are paid on the official government website or at the visa centre, never into an agent's personal bank account.
  • ✕"Embassy contact" or "inside person"Nobody can influence an embassy decision. People who claim this are selling lies.
  • ✕US lottery "processing fee"Entering the US Diversity Visa lottery is free on the official State Department site. Anyone charging to enter or "win" it is a scammer.
  • ✕Pressure to pay today"Only 2 slots left" and "price goes up tomorrow" are pressure tactics. Real routes have published dates.
  • ✕Fake or edited documentsNever use a fake bank statement, admission letter or job offer. Getting caught brings a 10-year UK ban and a record in other countries.
