New Zealand now refuses one in three student visa applications

Immigration New Zealand turned down 34% of full-fee student visa applications in July to September 2026, up from 13% over the previous year. It refused 6,273 in those three months alone, almost as many as in the whole of 2025-26. INZ says it found altered or reused bank documents, often linked to agents, and named Nigeria among countries where some refused files raised fraud concerns. Read more

GermanyFraud

No agentsGerman missions in Nigeria say they work with no visa agents

The German Embassy in Abuja and Consulate in Lagos warned Nigerians not to pay anyone promising a guaranteed visa, a job or a faster appointment. Booking an appointment is free, and only German officials decide visas. The embassy also said its online Consular Services Portal is only for certain long-stay national visas, not every application, despite what some agents claim.

UKFraud

Joint crackdownUK and Nigeria agree tougher action on visa fraud and fake documents

At their annual migration and home affairs talks in London, the two governments agreed to work together on visa fraud, organised immigration crime, document checks, extradition and the return of migrants. The UK says Nigeria is its third-biggest source of student visas and fifth-biggest for work visas. Both sides say the aim is to protect genuine travellers while punishing abuse.

Clock tickingUK graduate visa shrinks to 18 months on 1 January

Bachelor's and master's graduates who apply on or after 1 January 2027 get 18 months instead of 2 years. It is the application date that counts. PhD graduates keep 3 years.

CanadaRule change

Points shake-upCanada Express Entry rewards high-wage jobs from 3 November

From 3 November 2026, Express Entry gives new points for a qualifying job offer or at least a year of Canadian work in a high-wage occupation, worth up to 100 points for an offer. A job offer will no longer need an LMIA to count, though IRCC will check it is genuine. Scores in the pool will be recalculated and draws will pause for up to two weeks while that happens.
