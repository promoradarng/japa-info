Agent warning

German missions warn Nigerians: we don't work with visa agents

The German embassy in Abuja and consulate in Lagos said on X that they use no visa agents and that nobody can promise approval, a job or a faster slot. Booking an appointment through the official system costs nothing. The warning followed reports of applicants who paid large sums for fake offers. Read more

UKFraud

Fraud crackdownUK and Nigeria vow tougher action on visa fraud and fake documents

After talks in London on 6 October, UK and Nigerian officials agreed to work together on visa fraud, organised immigration crime and better document checks. They also pledged to support safe returns of people who abuse the system. No change to UK visa rules for ordinary Nigerian travellers was announced.

CanadaTiming

Longer waitsCanada visitor visa wait for Nigerians climbs to 103 days

IRCC's latest processing estimates put visitor visas from Nigeria at about 103 days, up from roughly 84 to 87 days a month earlier. Study permits from Nigeria also slipped, from 9 weeks to 10, while work permits held at 10 weeks. IRCC says these are estimates, not promises.

UKStudents

Visa limboUK student visa delays leave applicants missing course start dates

Universities report students stuck waiting for visas well beyond the three-week standard, with some cases taking up to four months and even the £500 priority service affected. Study visa applications fell 19% to 352,500 in the year to September 2026. The Home Office says straightforward applications are still being decided on time.

NigeriaFraud

Visa trapPolice free 11 Ghanaians held in Ogun after fake visa promise

Ogun police say they rescued 11 Ghanaians in Sagamu on 19 October who had been lured to Nigeria with promises of Canadian and Spanish visas. Each reportedly paid about GH₵40,000 before being held against their will. Detectives are still hunting the rest of the network.

UKStudents

Clock tickingUK graduate visa shrinks to 18 months on 1 January

Bachelor's and master's graduates who apply on or after 1 January 2027 get 18 months instead of 2 years. It is the application date that counts. PhD graduates keep 3 years.

UK · 16 Oct 2026Read more
CanadaRule change

Points shake-upCanada Express Entry rewards high-wage jobs from 3 November

From 3 November 2026, Express Entry gives new points for a qualifying job offer or at least a year of Canadian work in a high-wage occupation, worth up to 100 points for an offer. A job offer will no longer need an LMIA to count, though IRCC will check it is genuine. Scores in the pool will be recalculated and draws will pause for up to two weeks while that happens.
