Court don rule

US court strikes down four-year cap on student visa stays

A federal judge in Massachusetts has thrown out the Homeland Security rule that would have given F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors and foreign journalists a fixed admission period instead of 'duration of status'. The court had already paused the rule in September, and on 16 November it cancelled it completely. For now, students can stay as long as they keep studying full time on a valid I-20, but the government can still appeal. Read more

UKVisa warning

No guaranteeBritish High Commission warns Nigerians: nobody can guarantee a UK visa

The British High Commission in Nigeria has told applicants that no agent, person or company can promise a successful UK visa. It says any offer of guaranteed approval by email, phone call or social media is a scam. Applications should only be made through the official GOV.UK website.

UK · 16 Nov 2026Read more
AustraliaRule change

Family door closeAustralia stops graduates adding family to 485 visas later

From 13 November 2026, partners and children can only be included in a Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) visa if they apply together with the graduate in one combined application. In most cases they must already have been on the graduate's Student or 485 visa as dependants, or be a child born after that visa was granted; joining later as a subsequent entrant is no longer possible. Applications lodged before 13 November are decided under the old rules.

Australia · 13 Nov 2026Read more
AustraliaRule change

Apply from outsideAustralia's Training visa (407) now only for applicants outside Australia

From 12 November 2026, a new application for Australia's Training visa (subclass 407) is only valid if you lodge it while outside Australia, and you must also be outside Australia when the visa is granted. An application made from inside Australia under the new rule is treated as invalid and gets no bridging visa, even if the online system accepts it. Valid applications lodged before 12 November are still decided under the old rules, and existing 407 visas are not cancelled.

Australia · 12 Nov 2026Read more
UKStudents

Numbers dropUK student visa applications down 20% in a year, care worker applications down 61%

Home Office figures released on 12 November show 349,900 student main applications in the year to October 2026, 20% fewer than a year earlier, while student dependant applications fell 32%. Skilled Worker applications dropped 42% and Health and Care Worker applications fell 61%. The cuts follow tighter rules on dependants, salaries and overseas care recruitment.

UK · 12 Nov 2026Read more
CanadaRule change

Queue don moveCanada sets aside 45,000 extra PR places for spouses of Canadians in 2027

IRCC says it will use extra permanent residence spaces in 2027 to clear applications that are already waiting, including up to 45,000 for spouses, common-law partners and dependent children of Canadian citizens and permanent residents who live in Canada. A further 100,000 places will go to workers already in Canadian communities, chosen with the provinces. No new programme is opening, and people who have already applied do not need to contact IRCC.

Canada · 10 Nov 2026Read more
New ZealandRule change

Pikin firstNew Zealand won't grant parents residence while a child is unlawful

Immigration New Zealand says that for residence applications lodged from 12 November 2026, a parent or other secondary applicant cannot be approved while a dependent child is in the country without a valid visa. The child must first get a proper visa or leave New Zealand, and dropping the child from the application does not get around the rule. Applications lodged before 12 November are decided under the old rules.
