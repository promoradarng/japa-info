Numbers drop

UK student visa applications down 20% in a year, care worker applications down 61%

Home Office figures released on 12 November show 349,900 student main applications in the year to October 2026, 20% fewer than a year earlier, while student dependant applications fell 32%. Skilled Worker applications dropped 42% and Health and Care Worker applications fell 61%. The cuts follow tighter rules on dependants, salaries and overseas care recruitment. Read more

What changed this week

CanadaRule change

Queue don moveCanada sets aside 45,000 extra PR places for spouses of Canadians in 2027

IRCC says it will use extra permanent residence spaces in 2027 to clear applications that are already waiting, including up to 45,000 for spouses, common-law partners and dependent children of Canadian citizens and permanent residents who live in Canada. A further 100,000 places will go to workers already in Canadian communities, chosen with the provinces. No new programme is opening, and people who have already applied do not need to contact IRCC.

New ZealandRule change

Pikin firstNew Zealand won't grant parents residence while a child is unlawful

Immigration New Zealand says that for residence applications lodged from 12 November 2026, a parent or other secondary applicant cannot be approved while a dependent child is in the country without a valid visa. The child must first get a proper visa or leave New Zealand, and dropping the child from the application does not get around the rule. Applications lodged before 12 November are decided under the old rules.

CanadaRule change

Village routeCanada makes its rural immigration route to PR permanent

Canada has turned its Rural Community Immigration Pilot into a permanent programme, with 5,500 admission spaces planned in the 2027-2029 levels plan. The 14 current communities, including Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Brandon and Moose Jaw, stay in, and more will be added later. Candidates still need a job offer from an employer the community has designated, plus a community recommendation.

GermanyTiming

More slotsGermany's Lagos consulate to add visa counters before year end

Germany's Consul General in Lagos, Daniel Krull, said on 6 November that the consulate will open more visa counters before the end of 2026 and work with outside service providers to release more appointments. More files will also be sent to a German processing centre in Europe to cut delays. A document checking centre for Nigerians applying to German universities is planned in Lagos, but no date was given.

AustraliaStudents

Money wahalaAustralia may make students park funds in an Australian bank first

Australia's Home Affairs department is consulting on tougher Genuine Student rules and new proof-of-funds options. Three of the four options would make applicants hold money in an Australian bank account in their own name before applying, from half of total course and living costs up to a full first year. No decision has been made; submissions close on 23 November 2026.

Australia · 5 Nov 2026Read more
CanadaRule change

Same numbersCanada keeps 2027 targets: 380,000 new PRs, 150,000 new students

Canada's new immigration plan, released with the federal budget on 5 November, holds permanent resident admissions at 380,000 a year for 2027 to 2029. New temporary arrivals are capped at 370,000 a year, split into 220,000 workers and 150,000 students. Ottawa also plans to give permanent status to many people already in Canada on temporary permits.
