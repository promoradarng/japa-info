UK student visa funds go up on 30 November

From Sunday 30 November, UK student visa applicants must show more money for living costs: £1,570 a month for study in London (up from £1,529) and £1,203 a month elsewhere (up from £1,171), for up to nine months. The change comes from the Home Office's September statement of changes (HC 584). Applications made before 30 November are judged on the old amounts. Read more

NigeriaRule change

Made in NaijaNigeria to print its own passport booklets from May 2027

Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo broke ground on 20 November on a $70 million passport booklet factory at the Nigeria Immigration Service headquarters in Abuja, and said local production should start by May 2027. The plant is meant to make six million e-passport booklets a year at first, well above Nigeria's yearly need of about two million, so booklet shortages caused by foreign suppliers and exchange rates should ease. It is privately funded under a concession, and the new factory alone does not promise faster processing.

New ZealandTiming

System go slowNew Zealand visa portal down Sunday after Microsoft outage pushed upgrade back a week

Immigration New Zealand says many online visa applications, including student, most visitor, AEWV and partner or child of a worker or student visas, cannot be started or continued from 08:00 to 23:59 NZ time on Sunday 22 November. Some others, including partner or child of a New Zealander visas, go offline briefly between 06:00 and 08:00 on Monday 23 November. The upgrade was first planned for 15–16 November but slipped a week after a global Microsoft outage disrupted INZ systems.

UKVisa denial

Prepare well oUK judged 84% of interviewed Nigerian students not credible

A UK Visas and Immigration briefing seen by The PIE News shows caseworkers found 9,043 of the 11,415 student visa applicants they interviewed between May and October 2026 not credible. For Nigerian applicants the figure was 84%, behind only Nepal and India, and the share of Nigerians called for interview jumped from 7% in 2025 to 21% this year. The most common weak points were little research into the university, not explaining why the UK and the course fit future plans, poor course knowledge, English level and doubts about funds.

CanadaRule change

Postdoc pathCanada counts stipend-paid professor and postdoc work for Express Entry

IRCC has changed the Express Entry category for researchers with Canadian work experience. Work as a university professor, lecturer or postdoctoral researcher (NOC 41200) now counts even when it is paid through stipends, grants, bursaries or scholarships, and it can earn CRS points. Teaching and research assistant jobs (NOC 41201) no longer qualify for this category.

GermanyVisa warning

No buy ticket yetGerman mission: you don't need a paid flight ticket to apply for a visa

The German Mission in Nigeria said on X on 18 November that applicants do not have to buy a flight ticket before applying for a German visa. Depending on the visa checklist, a flight reservation or a proposed travel itinerary is enough. The post was the last in its series busting claims made by visa agents.

UKStudents

Free master'sCommonwealth Shared Scholarships for UK master's close 8 December

Applications are open for the 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarships, which pay for a one-year master's at a UK university starting in September or October 2027, and Nigerians are eligible. The award covers full tuition, a return flight and a monthly living allowance of £1,712, or £2,000 in the London area. The closing time is 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026.
