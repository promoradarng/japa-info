More Nigerian UK study applications withdrawn as universities fear refusals

A report citing a Financial Times analysis of Home Office data says 22.7% of Nigerian student visa applications were withdrawn in the first half of 2026, up from 4.5% a year earlier. The report links the jump to universities pulling offers early, because they must now keep visa refusals under 5% or risk losing their licence. The figures are secondhand, so treat them as a trend, not an official count. Read more

Passport ExtensionNigeria extends UK passport clinics to 27 November

The Nigeria Immigration Service has pushed its special passport exercise in the UK from 30 October to 27 November 2026. Citizenship goneUS court strips Nigerian-born woman of citizenship over passport lie

A US federal court in California has revoked the American citizenship of a Nigerian-born woman who was naturalised in 2013. The Justice Department said she had used another person's identity to get a US passport years earlier and lied about it during her citizenship interview. Officials say the case is part of a wider push to take back citizenship obtained through fraud.

Agent warningGerman missions warn Nigerians: we don't work with visa agents

The German embassy in Abuja and consulate in Lagos said on X that they use no visa agents and that nobody can promise approval, a job or a faster slot. Booking an appointment through the official system costs nothing. The warning followed reports of applicants who paid large sums for fake offers.

Fraud crackdownUK and Nigeria vow tougher action on visa fraud and fake documents

After talks in London on 6 October, UK and Nigerian officials agreed to work together on visa fraud, organised immigration crime and better document checks. They also pledged to support safe returns of people who abuse the system. No change to UK visa rules for ordinary Nigerian travellers was announced.
