More slots

Germany's Lagos consulate to add visa counters before year end

Germany's Consul General in Lagos, Daniel Krull, said on 6 November that the consulate will open more visa counters before the end of 2026 and work with outside service providers to release more appointments. More files will also be sent to a German processing centre in Europe to cut delays. A document checking centre for Nigerians applying to German universities is planned in Lagos, but no date was given. Read more

AustraliaStudents

Money wahalaAustralia may make students park funds in an Australian bank first

Australia's Home Affairs department is consulting on tougher Genuine Student rules and new proof-of-funds options. Three of the four options would make applicants hold money in an Australian bank account in their own name before applying, from half of total course and living costs up to a full first year. No decision has been made; submissions close on 23 November 2026.

CanadaRule change

Same numbersCanada keeps 2027 targets: 380,000 new PRs, 150,000 new students

Canada's new immigration plan, released with the federal budget on 5 November, holds permanent resident admissions at 380,000 a year for 2027 to 2029. New temporary arrivals are capped at 370,000 a year, split into 220,000 workers and 150,000 students. Ottawa also plans to give permanent status to many people already in Canada on temporary permits.

USARule change

New formUS now requires a health declaration from every air passenger

From 3 November, everyone flying to the United States, whatever their nationality, must sign a CDC form saying they have not been in the Democratic Republic of the Congo in the previous 21 days. Airlines must check the form before boarding, and it also applies to people connecting through a US airport. The order is set to run until 18 December unless CDC changes it.

UKRule change

Long waitUK sets 10 years as the normal wait for permanent settlement

The Home Secretary told Parliament on 2 November that most migrants will now wait ten years before settling, up from five. Higher earners above £50,271 and some public service workers can still settle after five years, and the top earners after three. Applicants must also show clean records, strong English, no public debts and steady National Insurance payments. No start date was given in the announcement.

UK · 2 Nov 2026Read more
UKStudents

Grad Visa LifelineUK Budget plans to keep two-year Graduate visa for some universities' students

The 28 October Budget says graduates of universities with strong immigration compliance and good job outcomes will keep a two-year Graduate visa, softening the planned cut to 18 months from 1 January 2027. The government has not yet said which universities qualify, what counts as good outcomes, or when this starts. Until rules are published, the 18-month cut stays the working assumption for applications made from 1 January.

UK · 28 Oct 2026Read more
UKStudents

Offer WahalaMore Nigerian UK study applications withdrawn as universities fear refusals

A report citing a Financial Times analysis of Home Office data says 22.7% of Nigerian student visa applications were withdrawn in the first half of 2026, up from 4.5% a year earlier. The report links the jump to universities pulling offers early, because they must now keep visa refusals under 5% or risk losing their licence. The figures are secondhand, so treat them as a trend, not an official count.
