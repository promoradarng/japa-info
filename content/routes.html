Visa routes

Which route fits you?

Six routes Nigerians ask about most, with where each one stands right now. Rules change often, so always confirm on the official government page before you pay anybody.

🇬🇧 UK Skilled Worker visa

Harder
Salary floor
£41,700 a year, or the job's going rate if higher
Lower rate
£33,400 for Immigration Salary List jobs, new entrants under 26, recent UK graduates
Skill level
Degree-level jobs (RQF 6) for most roles

You need a real job offer from a licensed UK sponsor. Nobody can "sell" you a sponsorship.

🇬🇧 UK Care Worker visa

Closed

Overseas recruitment for care workers and senior care workers closed on 22 July 2025. Anyone in Nigeria offering you a "care job sponsorship" for money is lying.

Nurses and other registered health roles still use the Health and Care route.

🇨🇦 Canada Study permit

Open
Living funds
CAD 23,448 for one person (outside Quebec), plus tuition and travel
Since
Applications made on or after 1 Sept 2026

Officers now check where your money came from in every case. Sudden big deposits get people refused.

🇨🇦 Canada Express Entry (PR)

Competitive

Points-based permanent residence. 2026 draws favour healthcare, trades, French speakers and people already in Canada. A strong IELTS or CELPIP score is the fastest way to raise your points.
