What to write to us about

  • Corrections: tell us the page and what is wrong, with a link to the official source if you have one.
  • News tips: visa rule changes, scams or problems Nigerians are facing abroad.
  • Advertising and partnerships: schools, IELTS centres, money-transfer services and licensed advisers who want to reach our readers.

Please note

We can't give advice on individual visa cases, check documents or help with applications. For your own case, contact a regulated immigration adviser or the official immigration authority of the country you are applying to. We will never ask you for money, passport details or bank details.
