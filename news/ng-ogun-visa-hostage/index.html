Visa trap

Police free 11 Ghanaians held in Ogun after fake visa promise

Nigeria · Fraud · 20 Oct 2026

NigeriaFraud

Ogun police say they rescued 11 Ghanaians in Sagamu on 19 October who had been lured to Nigeria with promises of Canadian and Spanish visas. Each reportedly paid about GH₵40,000 before being held against their will. Detectives are still hunting the rest of the network.

Japa wise

Never travel to meet an agent who promises a visa; real visas are applied for online and at official visa centres.

Grad Visa LifelineUK Budget plans to keep two-year Graduate visa for some universities' students

The 28 October Budget says graduates of universities with strong immigration compliance and good job outcomes will keep a two-year Graduate visa, softening the planned cut to 18 months from 1 January 2027. Offer WahalaMore Nigerian UK study applications withdrawn as universities fear refusals

A report citing a Financial Times analysis of Home Office data says 22.7% of Nigerian student visa applications were withdrawn in the first half of 2026, up from 4.5% a year earlier. The report links the jump to universities pulling offers early, because they must now keep visa refusals under 5% or risk losing their licence. The figures are secondhand, so treat them as a trend, not an official count.
