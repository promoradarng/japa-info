Visa trap

Police free 11 Ghanaians held in Ogun after fake visa promise

Nigeria · Fraud · 20 Oct 2026

NigeriaFraud

Ogun police say they rescued 11 Ghanaians in Sagamu on 19 October who had been lured to Nigeria with promises of Canadian and Spanish visas. Each reportedly paid about GH₵40,000 before being held against their will. Detectives are still hunting the rest of the network.
