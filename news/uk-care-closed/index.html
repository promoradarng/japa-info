Scam alert

Care worker visa from abroad is closed, but agents still sell it

UK · Fraud · 15 May 2026

UKFraud

The UK stopped sponsoring care workers and senior care workers from overseas on 22 July 2025. Any 'care job with sponsorship' offered to someone in Nigeria for a fee is fake.

Japa wise

Never pay for a job offer or a Certificate of Sponsorship.
