Fixed stay

US ends open-ended student stay: F-1 degree students get 4 years

USA · Students · 15 Sep 2026

USAStudents

A US Homeland Security rule that took effect on 15 September 2026 replaces the old 'duration of status' with a fixed end date on the I-94 record. Degree students get up to four years and language students up to 24 months. Staying longer means applying to USCIS for an extension, and a refusal can leave a student with no grace period.

Japa wise

Check the admit-until date on your I-94 after every entry, and plan long courses, like PhDs, with an extension in mind.
