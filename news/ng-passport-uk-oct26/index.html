Passport Rush

Last days: Nigeria passport clinics in UK close 30 October

Nigeria · Timing · 26 Oct 2026

NigeriaTiming

The Nigeria Immigration Service and the High Commission in London are running a second special passport exercise until 30 October 2026. Sessions hold in Manchester, London, Cardiff and Aberdeen, and you must book an appointment online first. Bring your old passport booklet for any renewal, reissue or data change, plus your payment receipt and appointment slip.

Japa wise

If your passport expires within six months, book a slot now and check the High Commission page for the full list of documents.
