New Zealand refuses 34% of student visas, names Nigeria in fraud warning

New Zealand · Visa denial · 15 Oct 2026

New ZealandVisa denial

Between July and September 2026, Immigration New Zealand turned down about a third of full-fee student visa applications, against 13% the year before. The agency says it found altered and reused documents, and named Nigeria among five countries where it saw fraud concerns, with master's courses checked hardest. It stressed this is about individual applications, not every Nigerian applicant.
