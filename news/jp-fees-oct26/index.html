Fee rise

Japan raises residence permit fees, permanent residence now ¥200,000

Japan · Rule change · 1 Oct 2026

JapanRule change

From 1 October 2026, Japan charges more for extending a stay, changing status and permanent residence. Extensions and status changes now cost between ¥10,000 and ¥75,000 depending on how long the stay granted is, while permanent residence jumps from ¥10,000 to ¥200,000. Certificates of Eligibility for new arrivals stay free.

Japa wise

Budget for the new fee tier before you renew, and confirm the exact amount on the Immigration Services Agency website.

Source: Metropolis Japan. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.
