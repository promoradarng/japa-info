Abuja shut

US visas for Nigerians now processed only in Lagos

USA · Rule change · 1 Aug 2026

USARule change

Routine US visa services at the Abuja embassy ended on 1 August 2026. Appointments, fees and interviews all go through the Lagos consulate. The partial visa suspension, visa bonds and immigrant visa pauses remain in force.

Japa wise

Book through the official US visa site only. Anyone selling 'Abuja slots' is a scammer.

Source: BusinessDay. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

A report citing a Financial Times analysis of Home Office data says 22.7% of Nigerian student visa applications were withdrawn in the first half of 2026, up from 4.5% a year earlier.
