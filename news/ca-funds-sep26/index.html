Canada raises student funds as money proof tops refusals

Canada · Visa denial · 10 Sep 2026

CanadaVisa denial

Study permit applicants outside Quebec must now show CAD 23,448 for living costs, plus tuition and travel. A study of June 2026 decisions found 47% of refusals came down to weak financial documents.

Points shake-upCanada Express Entry rewards high-wage jobs from 3 November

From 3 November 2026, Express Entry gives new points for a qualifying job offer or at least a year of Canadian work in a high-wage occupation, worth up to 100 points for an offer. Grad Visa LifelineUK Budget plans to keep two-year Graduate visa for some universities' students

The 28 October Budget says graduates of universities with strong immigration compliance and good job outcomes will keep a two-year Graduate visa, softening the planned cut to 18 months from 1 January 2027.
