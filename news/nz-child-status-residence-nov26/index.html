New Zealand won't grant parents residence while a child is unlawful

New Zealand · Rule change · 10 Nov 2026

New ZealandRule change

Immigration New Zealand says that for residence applications lodged from 12 November 2026, a parent or other secondary applicant cannot be approved while a dependent child is in the country without a valid visa. The child must first get a proper visa or leave New Zealand, and dropping the child from the application does not get around the rule. Applications lodged before 12 November are decided under the old rules.

Japa wise

If any child in your family has an expired New Zealand visa, sort out their status before you lodge a residence application.

Source: Immigration New Zealand. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Immigration New Zealand will start accepting a new Short-Term Graduate Work Visa on 16 November 2026.
