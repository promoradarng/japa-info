Portal move

Australia's SkillSelect moves into ImmiAccount on 28 November

Australia · Timing · 18 Nov 2026

AustraliaTiming

Home Affairs has taken SkillSelect offline from 24 to 27 November while it moves into ImmiAccount, with the new system going live on 28 November. Submitted expressions of interest for 189, 190 and 491 skilled visas stay valid, and invitations still inside their 60-day window can be opened in the new portal. Unsubmitted draft EOIs cannot be carried over.

Japa wise

If you only had a draft EOI, create an ImmiAccount and start a fresh EOI from 28 November; if yours was already submitted, import it to keep track of it.

Source: Department of Home Affairs / EIG Law. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Travel wahalaAustralia tightens travel permission for sponsored workers waiting on visas

Home Affairs says Bridging Visa B applications lodged from 30 November 2026 by people waiting on an employer-sponsored visa will get travel periods that match the actual trip, not the long periods of up to a year or more that were common. Applicants must give a real reason, dates and evidence, and a general 'holiday' or 'visiting family' line may not be enough. The law is not changing, only how strictly officers apply it, and a trip that runs past the approved period ends the BVB.

No switchingAustralia visitor visas lodged from 28 November will carry 'No Further Stay'

From 28 November 2026, condition 8503 ('No Further Stay') becomes compulsory on Australian Visitor (subclass 600) tourist and business visas, as well as ETA and eVisitor visas. Anyone holding such a visa will generally be unable to apply for another visa, such as a student or work visa, while inside Australia. It only covers applications lodged on or after 28 November, and a waiver is possible only after a major change in circumstances you could not have foreseen.

Family door closeAustralia stops graduates adding family to 485 visas later

From 13 November 2026, partners and children can only be included in a Temporary Graduate (subclass 485) visa if they apply together with the graduate in one combined application. In most cases they must already have been on the graduate's Student or 485 visa as dependants, or be a child born after that visa was granted; joining later as a subsequent entrant is no longer possible. Applications lodged before 13 November are decided under the old rules.
