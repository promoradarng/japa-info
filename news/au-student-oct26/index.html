New rules

Australia stops most onshore switches to student visas

Australia · Students · 2 Oct 2026

AustraliaStudents

From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders can't apply for a student visa inside Australia, and family members can't be added to a new student visa except in limited cases. The graduate visa fee rose to A$5,750 in July.

Japa wise

Plan to apply from Nigeria, and decide early whether your family is coming with you.

UKRule change

Long waitUK sets 10 years as the normal wait for permanent settlement

The Home Secretary told Parliament on 2 November that most migrants will now wait ten years before settling, up from five. Higher earners above £50,271 and some public service workers can still settle after five years, and the top earners after three. Applicants must also show clean records, strong English, no public debts and steady National Insurance payments. No start date was given in the announcement.

Grad Visa LifelineUK Budget plans to keep two-year Graduate visa for some universities' students

The 28 October Budget says graduates of universities with strong immigration compliance and good job outcomes will keep a two-year Graduate visa, softening the planned cut to 18 months from 1 January 2027. The government has not yet said which universities qualify, what counts as good outcomes, or when this starts. Until rules are published, the 18-month cut stays the working assumption for applications made from 1 January.
