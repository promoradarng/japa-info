UK graduate visa shrinks to 18 months on 1 January

UK · Students · 16 Oct 2026

UKStudents

Bachelor's and master's graduates who apply on or after 1 January 2027 get 18 months instead of 2 years. It is the application date that counts. PhD graduates keep 3 years.

Japa wise

Finished your degree this year? Submit your Graduate visa application before 31 December.
