Nigeria to print its own passport booklets from May 2027

Nigeria · Rule change · 20 Nov 2026

NigeriaRule change

Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo broke ground on 20 November on a $70 million passport booklet factory at the Nigeria Immigration Service headquarters in Abuja, and said local production should start by May 2027. The plant is meant to make six million e-passport booklets a year at first, well above Nigeria's yearly need of about two million, so booklet shortages caused by foreign suppliers and exchange rates should ease. It is privately funded under a concession, and the new factory alone does not promise faster processing.

Japa wise

Don't wait for the new factory: if your passport expires within the next year, renew now through the NIS contactless portal, because many embassies want at least six months' validity.
