UK Budget plans to keep two-year Graduate visa for some universities' students

UK · Students · 28 Oct 2026

UKStudents

The 28 October Budget says graduates of universities with strong immigration compliance and good job outcomes will keep a two-year Graduate visa, softening the planned cut to 18 months from 1 January 2027. The government has not yet said which universities qualify, what counts as good outcomes, or when this starts. Until rules are published, the 18-month cut stays the working assumption for applications made from 1 January.

Japa wise

If you finish your course soon, apply for the Graduate visa before 31 December 2026 to lock in two years, and ask your university about its compliance status before you pay a deposit.

Source: Times Higher Education. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Prepare well oUK judged 84% of interviewed Nigerian students not credible

A UK Visas and Immigration briefing seen by The PIE News shows caseworkers found 9,043 of the 11,415 student visa applicants they interviewed between May and October 2026 not credible. For Nigerian applicants the figure was 84%, behind only Nepal and India, and the share of Nigerians called for interview jumped from 7% in 2025 to 21% this year. The most common weak points were little research into the university, not explaining why the UK and the course fit future plans, poor course knowledge, English level and doubts about funds.

Free master'sCommonwealth Shared Scholarships for UK master's close 8 December

Applications are open for the 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarships, which pay for a one-year master's at a UK university starting in September or October 2027, and Nigerians are eligible.
