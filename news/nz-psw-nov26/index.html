New Zealand widens post-study work visa from 16 November

New Zealand · Students · 15 Oct 2026

New ZealandStudents

From 16 November 2026, people with a bachelor's degree plus a full-time New Zealand graduate diploma can qualify for a post-study work visa in their field. Students can already work 25 hours a week during term.

Japa wise

Pick a course that leads to work in a high-demand field if you want to stay after study.
