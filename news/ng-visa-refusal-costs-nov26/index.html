Money down drain

Nearly half of Nigerian Schengen visa applications refused in 2025

Europe · Visa denial · 2 Nov 2026

EuropeVisa denial

A Foreign Affairs ministry analysis reported by Sunday Punch and BusinessDay puts Nigeria's Schengen refusal rate at 46.9% in 2025, with Belgium, Spain and France refusing the most. Failed Schengen applications alone cost Nigerians about €11.5 million in fees that are never refunded. The same report puts UK refusals at 35.4% in early 2026 and Canadian study permit refusals at about 75%.

Japa wise

Before paying any visa fee, check that your bank statements, ties to Nigeria and travel plan clearly match the visa you are asking for.

CanadaRule change

Job offer don returnCanada's Express Entry gives job offers points again, no LMIA needed

Since 3 November, Express Entry has a new 'labour market integration' score worth up to 150 points. A job offer in a high-wage occupation now earns 50, 70 or 100 points depending on how well the job pays, and it no longer needs an LMIA; Canadian work experience in such jobs can score instead, but you get only the higher of the two. On 25 November IRCC invited 6,000 French speakers with a cut-off score of 401, the second draw under the new system.
