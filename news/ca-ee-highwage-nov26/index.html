Points shake-up

Canada Express Entry rewards high-wage jobs from 3 November

Canada · Rule change · 16 Oct 2026

CanadaRule change

From 3 November 2026, Express Entry gives new points for a qualifying job offer or at least a year of Canadian work in a high-wage occupation, worth up to 100 points for an offer. A job offer will no longer need an LMIA to count, though IRCC will check it is genuine. Scores in the pool will be recalculated and draws will pause for up to two weeks while that happens.

Japa wise

If you have a genuine high-wage Canadian job offer, update your Express Entry profile before 3 November.

Source: CIC News. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.
