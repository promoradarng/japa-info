Canada keeps 2027 targets: 380,000 new PRs, 150,000 new students

Canada · Rule change · 5 Nov 2026

CanadaRule change

Canada's new immigration plan, released with the federal budget on 5 November, holds permanent resident admissions at 380,000 a year for 2027 to 2029. New temporary arrivals are capped at 370,000 a year, split into 220,000 workers and 150,000 students. Ottawa also plans to give permanent status to many people already in Canada on temporary permits.

Village routeCanada makes its rural immigration route to PR permanent

Canada has turned its Rural Community Immigration Pilot into a permanent programme, with 5,500 admission spaces planned in the 2027-2029 levels plan. The 14 current communities, including Sudbury, Thunder Bay, Brandon and Moose Jaw, stay in, and more will be added later. Candidates still need a job offer from an employer the community has designated, plus a community recommendation.
