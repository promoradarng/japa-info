Money down drainNearly half of Nigerian Schengen visa applications refused in 2025

A Foreign Affairs ministry analysis reported by Sunday Punch and BusinessDay puts Nigeria's Schengen refusal rate at 46.9% in 2025, with Belgium, Spain and France refusing the most. Failed Schengen applications alone cost Nigerians about €11.5 million in fees that are never refunded. Numbers dropUK student visa applications down 20% in a year, care worker applications down 61%

Home Office figures released on 12 November show 349,900 student main applications in the year to October 2026, 20% fewer than a year earlier, while student dependant applications fell 32%. Skilled Worker applications dropped 42% and Health and Care Worker applications fell 61%. The cuts follow tighter rules on dependants, salaries and overseas care recruitment.

Queue don moveCanada sets aside 45,000 extra PR places for spouses of Canadians in 2027

IRCC says it will use extra permanent residence spaces in 2027 to clear applications that are already waiting, including up to 45,000 for spouses, common-law partners and dependent children of Canadian citizens and permanent residents who live in Canada. A further 100,000 places will go to workers already in Canadian communities, chosen with the provinces. No new programme is opening, and people who have already applied do not need to contact IRCC.
