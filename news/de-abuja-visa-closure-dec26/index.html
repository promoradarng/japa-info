Abuja shut

German embassy in Abuja closes visa section for December and January

Germany · Timing · 22 Oct 2026

View this post on X

Germany's embassy in Abuja says its visa section will be shut through December 2026 and January 2027 because of building work. During that time, visa applicants who would normally go to Abuja must apply at the German Consulate General in Lagos. No exact reopening date was given, and it is not clear whether Lagos will release extra appointments to cover the demand.

Japa wise

If you live in the North and need a German visa before February, book a Lagos appointment early and budget for the trip.
