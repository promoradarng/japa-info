Partial ban

Most US visitor and student visas suspended for Nigerians

USA · Visa denial · 1 Jan 2026

USAVisa denial

Since 1 January 2026, new B-1/B-2 visitor visas and F, M and J student and exchange visas are suspended for Nigerians without a valid US visa. Visas issued before then were not cancelled.

Japa wise

If you already hold a valid US visa, keep it valid and don't overstay it.

Source: The ICIR. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.
