Portal don shut

Ireland's work permit portal stays offline after security breach

Ireland · Timing · 9 Nov 2026

IrelandTiming

Ireland's Employment Permits Portal has been shut since late October after the Department of Enterprise found unauthorised activity on the system, and it has missed its planned reopening date. Applications already submitted are still being decided, but new applications cannot be sent by email while the department prepares temporary arrangements. Officials say people whose renewals or deadlines are delayed by the outage will get flexibility, and that only names, email addresses and account roles appear to have been exposed.

Japa wise

Watch for fake emails or texts about your permit: the department says it never asks for personal details that way, so send urgent questions only to epos@enterprise.gov.ie.

Source: Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment (Ireland). Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Pikin firstNew Zealand won't grant parents residence while a child is unlawful

Immigration New Zealand says that for residence applications lodged from 12 November 2026, a parent or other secondary applicant cannot be approved while a dependent child is in the country without a valid visa. The child must first get a proper visa or leave New Zealand, and dropping the child from the application does not get around the rule. Applications lodged before 12 November are decided under the old rules.
