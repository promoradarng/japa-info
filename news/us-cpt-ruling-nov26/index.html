Internship reprieve

US judge blocks new limits on student internships (CPT)

USA · Students · 17 Nov 2026

USAStudents

A federal judge in Boston has stopped the Department of Homeland Security from enforcing two August 2026 directives that narrowed curricular practical training (CPT), the work placements F-1 students do as part of their course. Under the directives, a placement only counted if every student in the programme had to do it, which shut out elective internships taken for credit. The order is a preliminary injunction, so the case continues and the government could still appeal.

Japa wise

If you are on an F-1 visa and a CPT placement was cancelled or put on hold, ask your school's international student office (your DSO) whether it can now be authorised again.

Court don ruleUS court strikes down four-year cap on student visa stays

A federal judge in Massachusetts has thrown out the Homeland Security rule that would have given F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors and foreign journalists a fixed admission period instead of 'duration of status'. The court had already paused the rule in September, and on 16 November it cancelled it completely.
