Passport Extension

Nigeria extends UK passport clinics to 27 November

Nigeria · Timing · 27 Oct 2026

NigeriaTiming

The Nigeria Immigration Service has pushed its special passport exercise in the UK from 30 October to 27 November 2026. London and Manchester run from 3 to 27 November, while Aberdeen and Cardiff only open from 3 to 6 November. Only people who already booked online are served, and cash is not accepted at the venues.

Japa wise

Book your slot on the High Commission portal now and bring your application form, payment slip, appointment printout, return envelope and old passport.

Offer WahalaMore Nigerian UK study applications withdrawn as universities fear refusals

A report citing a Financial Times analysis of Home Office data says 22.7% of Nigerian student visa applications were withdrawn in the first half of 2026, up from 4.5% a year earlier.
