School ban

Korea bars 20 universities from sponsoring student visas

Korea · Visa denial · 12 Feb 2026

KoreaVisa denial

Korea's education ministry stopped 20 universities and language programmes from issuing student visas for one year from fall 2026. Students admitted there cannot get a D-2 visa through them.

Japa wise

Before paying a deposit, ask the school in writing if it can sponsor visas this year.

Source: SeoulStart. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Court don ruleUS court strikes down four-year cap on student visa stays

A federal judge in Massachusetts has thrown out the Homeland Security rule that would have given F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors and foreign journalists a fixed admission period instead of 'duration of status'. The court had already paused the rule in September, and on 16 November it cancelled it completely.
