Canada tight

Canada approved 24% fewer visitor visas for Nigerians this year

Canada · Visa denial · 19 Nov 2026

CanadaVisa denial

New IRCC figures show Canada approved 26,210 visitor visas for Nigerians between January and August 2026, down from 34,360 in the same months of 2025, even though total approvals worldwide rose by about 11%. Nigeria still ranks fifth among source countries, and August alone was better than a year earlier. IRCC's latest estimate puts a visitor visa decision for applicants in Nigeria at about 98 days, while study permits take about 11 weeks.

Japa wise

Apply for a Canadian visitor visa at least four months before your trip, and show a clear reason to return home, such as a job, business or family ties, with documents to back it up.

Job offer don returnCanada's Express Entry gives job offers points again, no LMIA needed

Since 3 November, Express Entry has a new 'labour market integration' score worth up to 150 points. A job offer in a high-wage occupation now earns 50, 70 or 100 points depending on how well the job pays, and it no longer needs an LMIA; Canadian work experience in such jobs can score instead, but you get only the higher of the two. On 25 November IRCC invited 6,000 French speakers with a cut-off score of 401, the second draw under the new system.

Postdoc pathCanada counts stipend-paid professor and postdoc work for Express Entry

IRCC has changed the Express Entry category for researchers with Canadian work experience. Work as a university professor, lecturer or postdoctoral researcher (NOC 41200) now counts even when it is paid through stipends, grants, bursaries or scholarships, and it can earn CRS points. Teaching and research assistant jobs (NOC 41201) no longer qualify for this category.
