New form

US now requires a health declaration from every air passenger

USA · Rule change · 3 Nov 2026

USARule change

From 3 November, everyone flying to the United States, whatever their nationality, must sign a CDC form saying they have not been in the Democratic Republic of the Congo in the previous 21 days. Airlines must check the form before boarding, and it also applies to people connecting through a US airport. The order is set to run until 18 December unless CDC changes it.

Japa wise

Fill in the form at check-in or at the airport, and carry your travel history details in case the airline asks.

Internship reprieveUS judge blocks new limits on student internships (CPT)

A federal judge in Boston has stopped the Department of Homeland Security from enforcing two August 2026 directives that narrowed curricular practical training (CPT), the work placements F-1 students do as part of their course. Under the directives, a placement only counted if every student in the programme had to do it, which shut out elective internships taken for credit. The order is a preliminary injunction, so the case continues and the government could still appeal.

Fewer arrivalsNew international students at US universities down 15% this autumn

The Institute of International Education says US colleges hosted about 1.15 million international students in 2025/26, but its autumn 2026 snapshot of more than 800 institutions shows total international enrolment down 9% and new enrolment down 15%.
