Charter flight

Germany-led flight deports dozens of Nigerians from four European countries

Germany · Deportation · 27 Aug 2026

Deportation

Stock image: Payton Chung / Flickr, CC BY 2.0

A charter flight organised by Germany, with Austria, Luxembourg and Hungary taking part, returned dozens of Nigerians to Lagos in late August, along with a few Ghanaians and a Gambian. Most came from Germany. Germany has deported more Nigerians than any other EU country this year, with at least 137 removed in five flights between February and June.
