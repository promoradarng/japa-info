No guarantee

British High Commission warns Nigerians: nobody can guarantee a UK visa

UK · Visa warning · 16 Nov 2026

View this post on X

The British High Commission in Nigeria has told applicants that no agent, person or company can promise a successful UK visa. It says any offer of guaranteed approval by email, phone call or social media is a scam. Applications should only be made through the official GOV.UK website.

Japa wise

Apply only on GOV.UK, never pay anyone who promises approval, and report suspicious offers before sending money or documents.

Source: British High Commission Nigeria (via Legit.ng). Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Prepare well oUK judged 84% of interviewed Nigerian students not credible

A UK Visas and Immigration briefing seen by The PIE News shows caseworkers found 9,043 of the 11,415 student visa applicants they interviewed between May and October 2026 not credible. For Nigerian applicants the figure was 84%, behind only Nepal and India, and the share of Nigerians called for interview jumped from 7% in 2025 to 21% this year. The most common weak points were little research into the university, not explaining why the UK and the course fit future plans, poor course knowledge, English level and doubts about funds.
