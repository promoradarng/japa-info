Embassy shut

US Embassy Abuja and Lagos consulate shut 26–27 November

USA · Timing · 25 Nov 2026

USATiming

The US Mission in Nigeria says its embassy in Abuja and consulate in Lagos are closed on Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November for the American Thanksgiving holiday. No consular or visa appointments will be served on those two days, and normal work is expected to resume the following week.

Japa wise

If your interview or passport pickup fell on 26 or 27 November, check your appointment account and rebook through the official US visa portal only, not through an agent.

Source: Vanguard. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Court don ruleUS court strikes down four-year cap on student visa stays

A federal judge in Massachusetts has thrown out the Homeland Security rule that would have given F-1 students, J-1 exchange visitors and foreign journalists a fixed admission period instead of 'duration of status'. The court had already paused the rule in September, and on 16 November it cancelled it completely.
