More slots

Germany's Lagos consulate to add visa counters before year end

Germany · Timing · 6 Nov 2026

GermanyTiming

Germany's Consul General in Lagos, Daniel Krull, said on 6 November that the consulate will open more visa counters before the end of 2026 and work with outside service providers to release more appointments. More files will also be sent to a German processing centre in Europe to cut delays. A document checking centre for Nigerians applying to German universities is planned in Lagos, but no date was given.

Japa wise

Book German visa appointments only through the official consulate and service provider websites, and check often for newly released slots.
