Renew am now

Ireland tells residents travelling at Christmas to renew IRP cards early

Ireland · Timing · 9 Nov 2026

IrelandTiming

Immigration Service Delivery says Irish Residence Permit renewals are taking about six to nine weeks because of high demand, and the new card can take up to two more weeks to arrive. It cannot promise a card in time for holiday travel, and emergency re-entry visas are only for genuine emergencies, not planned trips. Your new permission still starts from the old card's expiry date, so applying early costs you nothing.

Japa wise

If your IRP card expires before or during a Christmas trip home, apply online for renewal today and keep your proof of application with you.

Source: Envoy Global / Immigration Service Delivery. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Job offer don returnCanada's Express Entry gives job offers points again, no LMIA needed

Since 3 November, Express Entry has a new 'labour market integration' score worth up to 150 points. A job offer in a high-wage occupation now earns 50, 70 or 100 points depending on how well the job pays, and it no longer needs an LMIA; Canadian work experience in such jobs can score instead, but you get only the higher of the two. On 25 November IRCC invited 6,000 French speakers with a cut-off score of 401, the second draw under the new system.
