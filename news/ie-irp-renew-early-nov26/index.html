Renew am now

Ireland tells residents travelling at Christmas to renew IRP cards early

Ireland · Timing · 9 Nov 2026

IrelandTiming

Immigration Service Delivery says Irish Residence Permit renewals are taking about six to nine weeks because of high demand, and the new card can take up to two more weeks to arrive. It cannot promise a card in time for holiday travel, and emergency re-entry visas are only for genuine emergencies, not planned trips. Your new permission still starts from the old card's expiry date, so applying early costs you nothing.

Japa wise

If your IRP card expires before or during a Christmas trip home, apply online for renewal today and keep your proof of application with you.

Source: Envoy Global / Immigration Service Delivery. Always confirm details on the official government website before you act.

Portal don shutIreland's work permit portal stays offline after security breach

Ireland's Employment Permits Portal has been shut since late October after the Department of Enterprise found unauthorised activity on the system, and it has missed its planned reopening date. Applications already submitted are still being decided, but new applications cannot be sent by email while the department prepares temporary arrangements. Officials say people whose renewals or deadlines are delayed by the outage will get flexibility, and that only names, email addresses and account roles appear to have been exposed.

Made in NaijaNigeria to print its own passport booklets from May 2027

Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo broke ground on 20 November on a $70 million passport booklet factory at the Nigeria Immigration Service headquarters in Abuja, and said local production should start by May 2027. The plant is meant to make six million e-passport booklets a year at first, well above Nigeria's yearly need of about two million, so booklet shortages caused by foreign suppliers and exchange rates should ease. It is privately funded under a concession, and the new factory alone does not promise faster processing.
